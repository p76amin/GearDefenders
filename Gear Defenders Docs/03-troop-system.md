# Troop System

**Confidence:** High for named roster & community roles; Medium for ability details (tier-list sites vary); Low for exact stats

---

## Design role of troops

Troops are the **spawned combatants** produced by troop gears on the board. Meta progression levels/stars them; in-battle merges raise the quality of individual gears spawning them. A typical warband is a **small focused loadout** (community often discusses ~4 active troops), not a full collection dump.

Core principle (Clashiverse): *“A smaller team of powerful, fast units outperforms a large mix of average ones.”*

## Combat roles (design taxonomy)

| Role | Job | Exemplars |
|------|-----|-----------|
| **Frontline** | Occupy space, absorb DPS, enable backline | Paladin, Heavy Guard, Shielder |
| **Primary DPS** | Delete priority targets / sustain damage | Ninja, Mage, Thunder Caller |
| **AoE / CC** | Clear packs, slow/control rushes | Arbalist, Paladin AoE, Ninja slow, Catapult |
| **Dive / disrupt** | Reach enemy ranged / bosses | Lancer, Cataphract, Elephant Knight |
| **Economy of presence** | Cheap fast spawns to stall | Shielder floods, early Spearmen |

## Merge / upgrade / promotion

### In-battle merge

- Merge **identical troop gears** to create higher-tier spawns.  
- Cluttering the board with unmerged basics slows effective power and wastes chicken legs.  
- “Super troops” (high merge results) pair especially well with **Mirror** (community).

### Out-of-battle progression

| Lever | Effect (public) | Confidence |
|-------|-----------------|------------|
| **Level** | Raises combat stats; Endless uses **average troop level** for scaling | High |
| **Stars** | Raise max level cap; boost ATK/HP | High (Clashiverse) |
| **Duplicate cards / materials** | Feed upgrades / promotions | High |
| **Focus advice** | Push top 2–4 to high levels (e.g. 40 cited) rather than spreading | High (Reddit) |

Exact level caps, star costs, and stat curves: **unknown**.

## Warband / loadout

Community-validated setups (treat as **meta snapshots**, not eternal truth):

| Stage | Common warband | Why |
|-------|----------------|-----|
| Early | Heavy Guard + Mage / Arbalist / Spearman | Tank + answer to heavies/packs |
| Optimal mid–late | **Paladin + Ninja** or **Paladin + Arbalist** | Best consensus carry pairs |
| Full 4-stack example | Paladin, Heavy Guard, Ninja, Arbalist | Tank + slow + pierce CC (late Reddit reports) |
| Alternate | Paladin, Arbalist/Ninja, Thunder Caller/Mage, Heavy Guard | Mixed magic |

**Strong community consensus:** If you own Paladin, build around Paladin; Ninja is the most praised ranged partner.

---

## Counterplay notes (partial)

- **Enemy Heavy** → Mage frequently recommended.  
- **Enemy Infantry packs** → Arbalist.  
- **Enemy Ranged / pierce** → Cavalry dive fantasy (Lancer/Cataphract), but AI reliability contested.  
- **One-shot late stages** → Heavy Guard ultimates + Ninja slows to buy time (Reddit late-game).
